# European Agent Research

## 🇩🇪 German Research

### DFKI (German Research Center for AI)

| Work | Year | Contribution |
|------|------|-------------|
| **AJAN Framework** | 2023 | SPARQL-extended Behavior Trees for Semantic Web agents, RDF knowledge modeling |
| **Agentic AI for Robot Control** | 2025 | LLM-based planning for Mobipick (indoor) + Valdemar (agricultural); demonstrates flexibility + fragility |
| **Experience-Based Learning Systems** | 2026 | Hybrid AI combining symbolic + subsymbolic; ProCAKE, CBRkit frameworks for agentic reasoning |
| **Trusted AI + Agents** | 2026 | "Third wave AI": neural + symbolic; "Trust by Design" for certification |

### Fraunhofer Institutes

| Work | Institution | Year | Contribution |
|------|------------|------|-------------|
| **Agentic AI Research** | IAIS | 2025 | Reliability, transparency, domain fidelity for law/medicine/engineering |
| **GenAI Multi-Agent Orchestration** | FIT | 2024 | Morphological box for MAS orchestration; 4 propositions for organizational MAS |
| **Multi-Agent Rule-Compliant Decisions** | FOKUS | 2024 | Three-agent system (proposing + norm ID + fact retrieval) with Prolog reasoner |

### Technical Universities

| Work | Institution | Year | Contribution |
|------|------------|------|-------------|
| **SAGE + OPACA** | TU Berlin | 2025 | Dynamic tool discovery framework; zero-shot prompting; multi-agent extensibility |
